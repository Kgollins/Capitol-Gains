Reading the map…

Each dot is a stock. A large gold dot had earnings, a news burst or a big price move that day. A coloured thread marks a ripple: unusually many linked stocks moved the same way within 2 trading days.

Key
  • Each dot is a stock. A large gold dot had earnings, a news burst or a big price move that day. A coloured thread marks a ripple: unusually many linked stocks moved the same way within 2 trading days.
  • quiet: normal attention (Wikipedia views and news)
  • loud: attention at least 2 times its 90-day level
  • tired: attention rose, then stalled or faded
  • held: in the paper account after that day's trades
  • no reading: no closing price that day
  • catalyst: earnings, a news burst, or a move of 2 usual daily moves or more
  • moved with it: a linked stock followed within 2 trading days
  • turned against it: it followed, then reversed
  • The map does not tell which of the same-day catalysts moved a linked stock.
  • fainter: ripples that arrived before the day shown
  • died out: no ripple within 5 trading days
  • pale threads: two stocks that are each other's closest links. Others show on hover or zoom.
  • the other catalysts' links: drawn when you pick one
