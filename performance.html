How to read the chart

Each line starts at 100 on the lane's first close, when its slice held only cash. A value of 95 means that the lane lost 5% of its slice. The Nasdaq-100 line starts at 100 on the first day of the record.

Pick a lane with its button or its row in the table to show it in colour. The other lanes stay grey. A lane with no trades yet holds only cash, so its line stays at 100.

Select a lane's name in the table to read what the lane does. Its description opens on this page.

In the table, "Against Nasdaq-100" is the lane's return minus the Nasdaq-100's return over the same days, in percentage points. "In stocks" is the part of the slice held in stocks at the last close.

During a trading day, the last point is the day so far: each lane's holdings at the latest prices, and QQQ at its latest price. It updates every 10 minutes. After the close, the lane's own mark of the close replaces it.

A few days of results cannot show skill. The Lanes page explains how the lab compares each lane with its random twins.
